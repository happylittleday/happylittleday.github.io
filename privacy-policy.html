Privacy Policy of Offline Games10000

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SITETYPE LIMITED (Sekai Web), the operator of Offline Games10000, also displayed as 10001 GAMES (the App), handles information. The free Android edition supports advertisements and an optional one-time purchase to remove advertisements. Game content can be played offline. Advertising, purchases and purchase restoration use an internet connection. The advertising and purchase sections apply to the 1.183.0 (358) advertising edition. Earlier offline editions and browser previews do not load these services.

Policy summary

  • Game records and settings are stored on your device. This offline edition does not automatically upload gameplay records to our servers and does not require an account.
  • The advertising edition uses Google AdMob, the Google User Messaging Platform and Google Play Billing. Ads are restricted and not personalised. Google may process device, network, advertising interaction and diagnostic information as explained below. No separate gameplay analytics service is included.
  • If you choose to share a score image or contact us by email, information is handled as explained below. App stores and the services you choose have their own privacy practices.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores information needed for its features on your device. This includes language, display and difficulty preferences; audio and vibration settings; favourites; scores and play history; progress, achievements and rewards; selected characters, outfits and any nickname you configure; and saved positions for games that support resuming.

These records are local app data, not an online account with us. We use them to remember your preferences, provide game features, display your records and resume supported games. We do not automatically receive these records. Not every game saves a session in progress.

2. Automatic collection, advertising and analytics

Playing does not require an account with us. Scores and settings are not automatically uploaded to our servers. The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise location access.

The free advertising edition includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). These services may process IP addresses, device and app information, app set identifiers and applicable account-related identifiers, app launches, advertising interactions and diagnostics to deliver advertisements, prevent fraud, measure advertising and manage privacy choices. An IP address may be used to estimate a general location. Google processes information under its privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and advertising information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

We request restricted, non-personalised advertisements with a general-audience content rating and child-directed and under-age-of-consent treatment. The Android build removes the advertising-ID permission. These restrictions do not mean that the ad services process no information. We do not add third-party mediation networks, a separate analytics SDK or an automatic gameplay crash-reporting SDK.

Advertisements are displayed in the game list, outside active gameplay. Privacy choices are handled by UMP where required. When Google requires an entry point for changing these choices, an advertising privacy-settings button is available in the advertising purchase screen. If ads cannot be requested, the games remain playable without an advertisement.

Google Play and the operating system may independently process download, update, review, purchase or diagnostic information under their own policies and your settings. The browser preview and builds without the advertising module do not load an advertising SDK.

3. Sharing a score image

Sharing is optional. If you use the share feature, the App creates an image that may include the game name, score, selected character and any configured nickname. It passes that image to the app or destination you select in the system share sheet. It does not automatically publish the image to us or to a social network.

Review the image and the destination before sending it. The recipient may save, transmit or share it again under its own terms and privacy policy. We cannot remove copies held by recipients.

On Android, a temporary sharing image is stored in a limited app cache area and the selected recipient receives permission to read that image. The image may remain after the share sheet closes. The App attempts to clear previous sharing images when creating a new one, and the operating system may also clear cached files.

4. Information you send when contacting us

If you email info@sekaiweb.com, we may receive your email address, the content of your message and any information or screenshots you choose to attach. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue, provide support and handle related follow-up. Please send only what is needed and avoid passwords, payment details or unnecessary information about other people.

Email is handled through email service providers. We may use service providers to receive and manage correspondence, with access limited to what is needed for that purpose. Email providers also handle information according to their own policies. Contacting us is optional and is not required to play.

5. Purposes and legal grounds

We handle information to provide the App's local features, carry out sharing you request, answer your enquiries, maintain support records and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information or use support correspondence for targeted advertising.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, the basis depends on the activity: providing a service you request, our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and supporting the App, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where it is required. If processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent by contacting us; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. Starting the game is not blanket consent to future advertising or tracking.

6. External services and disclosures

Advertising uses Google AdMob and UMP. Buying the optional ad-removal upgrade uses Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the transaction, account and payment details. We do not receive your card details. The App obtains a product identifier, purchase state, signed purchase receipt and purchase token so it can verify, acknowledge and restore the upgrade. The signed receipt is stored in private app storage for offline ad-removal access. Owned purchases are checked again through Google Play when a connection is available. Refunded or revoked purchases may no longer provide ad removal.

The advertising SDK may send the information described above to Google. The App does not send your local game history, nickname or score images to the ad network. Optional score sharing, app updates, reviews, support email and destinations you select involve their respective providers. Their policies also apply.

For information we actually hold, such as a support message, we may disclose information to providers needed to handle the request, to comply with a valid legal obligation, or where necessary to establish or defend legal claims. We do not have a central database of your local game records to disclose.

7. Storage, retention and security

Local records remain in the App's device storage for continued use until they are removed or replaced by the App, cleared through device settings, or otherwise removed by the operating system or uninstall process. The Android edition disables the App's automatic backup feature. We do not provide our own cloud account, cross-device synchronisation or remote recovery service.

Support correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably needed to handle the enquiry and related follow-up, resolve disputes or meet applicable legal obligations. When it is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymised. Retention may vary with the nature of the request and applicable requirements; we do not set a single fixed retention period for every message.

The App uses device app storage and limits Android sharing access to the chosen image. We limit access to support information to what is needed for its purpose. No device, storage method or communication service can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device protected and do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

8. Your choices and deletion

You can change supported preferences in Settings, including sound and vibration. To remove local App records, use your operating system's app-data controls or uninstall the App. This can erase scores, settings and saved progress, so check what you need before doing so. We cannot remotely retrieve, restore or selectively delete records stored only on your device.

This edition does not create an online account with us, so there is no online account to close. To request deletion of information you have sent to us, contact info@sekaiweb.com. Information retained by a sharing recipient, email provider, app store or a separate backup or transfer service must be addressed with that provider. Removal from the App does not remove copies held elsewhere.

Ad removal is a non-consumable, one-time Google Play purchase, not a subscription. Restore it with the same Google Play account. Clearing app data removes the local receipt cache but does not itself cancel the Play purchase. Refunds and store purchase records are managed through Google Play. Removing ads stops further in-app ad requests; it does not delete information previously processed by Google.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information we hold, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, withdraw consent or complain to a competent data protection authority. These rights apply subject to the conditions and exceptions in the relevant law.

Send requests or questions to info@sekaiweb.com. We will handle them in accordance with applicable law and may need information reasonably necessary to identify the request and verify your entitlement. We cannot supply information we do not hold or control information held independently by your device, app store or chosen recipients.

10. Children and guardians

The intended audience is age 13 and above, subject to local age requirements and the content rating assigned in each country. The App does not ask for a date of birth or create a child profile. Because user ages are not collected, all ad requests receive the restricted treatment described above. No personalised advertising is requested. Guardians should supervise purchases and sharing where required.

Parents and guardians should supervise sharing and support requests where appropriate. Do not include a child's full name, address, school or other unnecessary identifying information in shared images or email. If you believe a child has sent us personal information that should be removed, contact us so we can review and address it under applicable law.

11. International processing

Local game records are processed on your device. AdMob, UMP, Google Play, email and sharing services may process information internationally, including in countries where Google and its providers operate. Their policies describe their international processing and safeguards. For support information we handle, we use safeguards required by applicable law. Contact us about the handling of your enquiry.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or our information practices change. The latest revision date appears at the top. Material changes will be explained through an appropriate notice, and we will seek consent where required before starting new processing that requires it.

Material changes to advertising, purchases, recipients or information handling require revised disclosures and any consent required by applicable law. Starting to play or accepting the Terms is not blanket consent to personalised advertising or future tracking.
